# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The CEA Learning Demos project welcomes people who contribute, ask questions,
review work, or learn from the examples. Everyone should be able to take part
without harassment or intimidation, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing technical work.
- Explain disagreement with evidence and focus criticism on ideas or code.
- Welcome questions and corrections, including corrections to this document.
- Give actionable, kind feedback that helps others participate.
- Respect privacy and do not disclose another person's information.
- Use inclusive language and make room for different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or publishing
private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive
conduct after a request to stop may also violate this code.

Do not use project spaces to target or silence someone for raising a concern.
Critique of an algorithm, implementation, or decision is allowed when it
addresses the work respectfully.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, project
discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.
It also applies to conduct outside those spaces when it materially affects a
person's participation in the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
privately through the GitHub profile for Brajesh31. Do not post sensitive
details in a public issue. Include a short description, approximate time,
project space, and any context needed to understand the concern. Share only
information that is safe for you to provide.

## Response and enforcement

The maintainer will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical, protect
the reporter's privacy where possible, and give the involved people a chance
to provide relevant context. Depending on severity and pattern, actions may
include a warning, removal of harmful content, temporary participation
limits, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review
is not acceptable. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the
behavior and applied consistently. This project is maintained by volunteers;
a report may take time to review.

## Questions

For a private conduct concern, use the reporting route above. For routine
technical support, follow SUPPORT.md instead.
## Good-faith technical disagreement

A different view about code, algorithm definitions, or project priorities is
not misconduct by itself. Keep the discussion relevant, explain reasoning, and
avoid claims about another contributor's character or motives.
