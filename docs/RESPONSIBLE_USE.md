# 🧭 Responsible Use

These applications demonstrate simplified machine-learning concepts. They are not validated decision systems.

## Prohibited interpretation
Do not use outputs for hiring, education admissions, insurance, finance, credit, healthcare, law enforcement, public benefits or any decision that materially affects a person.

## Model limitations
- Candidate Elimination page has simplified, incomplete boundary updates.
- Neural-network page performs a single hard-coded teaching update and has no meaningful evaluation or retained training state.
- ID3-named page is only a majority-class dummy baseline, with in-sample accuracy.

## Data
Use synthetic examples. Do not enter personal, confidential or regulated information. Do not infer personal traits or make claims about real people from these demos.

## Communication
When sharing screenshots or results, state the limitations next to the output. Never present synthetic accuracy as evidence of real-world model quality.
